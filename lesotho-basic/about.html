About Lesotho

History, people and quick facts

History

Lesotho was founded by King Moshoeshoe I in the 1820s. He protected the Basotho people from his mountain fortress at Thaba Bosiu.

The country was called Basutoland under British rule and became independent on 4 October 1966.

Culture

The Basotho people are known for:

  • The colourful Basotho blanket
  • The mokorotlo (straw hat) shown on the flag
  • Pony riding and traditional music

Did you know?

Lesotho is the only country in the world where all the land is above 1,400 metres.
